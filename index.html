About Zhengzhou Ruixin Advertising Co., Ltd.

Zhengzhou Ruixin Advertising Co., Ltd. is a Chinese manufacturer and supplier of LED advertising light boxes. Based in Zhengzhou, Henan Province, we focus on a small, well-engineered product range: snap frame light boxes, slide-in standing light boxes and SEG fabric light boxes.

Every unit is built around a slim aluminum profile, high-quality LED modules and even, flicker-free illumination — ready to install on arrival.

Whether you run a single shop, a restaurant chain or an advertising agency, we offer factory-direct prices, custom dimensions and dependable export service. Send us your size, and we will take care of the rest.
